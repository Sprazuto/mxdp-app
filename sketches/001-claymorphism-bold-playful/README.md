## Variant: Bold Playful Claymorphism

### Design stance
High-contrast, colorful claymorphism with hard shadows — feels like a toy box spilled open. Energy-first, celebration of action.

### Key choices
- **Layout:** Centered but dense, pill-style topic chips, hard-drop shadows (offset layers)
- **Typography:** Baloo 2 (body) + Comic Neue (display) — chunky, expressive, playful
- **Color:** Vibrant coral, orange, teal, pink, blue, purple — saturated triadic palette
- **Interaction:** Hard shadow "pop" on hover/press, confetti explosion, wiggle animation, stagger bounce-in

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Immediate excitement, clear CTAs, memorability, older kids (6-10) energy
- Weak at: Calm contexts, extended reading, low-stimulation needs, dark mode (needs work)

### Best for
- Launch campaigns, app store screenshots, kids who want "game energy", birthday/party vibe

## Variant: Soft Dreamy Claymorphism

### Design stance
Gentle, pastel claymorphism with layered depth — feels like a warm hug. Prioritizes calm exploration over high energy.

### Key choices
- **Layout:** Centered single-column, generous whitespace, staged reveal animations
- **Typography:** Quicksand (body) + Fredoka (display) — rounded, friendly, highly legible
- **Color:** Warm cream base, peach/pink/mint/lavender/sky accents — soft analogous palette
- **Interaction:** Float animations, gentle hover lift, pressed states, confetti burst on CTA

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Calm trust, readability, accessibility (low motion respected), emotional warmth
- Weak at: High-energy "grab attention" moments, dense info hierarchy

### Best for
- Parents browsing with kids, bedtime/quiet time context, trust-first branding, younger kids (3-6)

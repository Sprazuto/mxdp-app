# Product Requirement Document (PRD)

## 1. Problem Statement
Traditional early education websites are often slow, difficult to maintain, or overly generic. The goal is to establish a vibrant, trustworthy, and ultra-fast public entry point that impresses young parents while serving as the foundational layer for future enterprise features.

## 2. Target Audience
- **Primary:** Prospective Parents (Ages 23–38) seeking quality early childhood education.
- **Secondary:** Internal School Admin & Teachers managing updates and inquiries.

## 3. Success Metrics (KPIs)
- **Performance:** 100/100 Lighthouse score on Mobile & Desktop.
- **Speed:** Sub-1 second initial page load time.
- **Conversion:** Streamlined inquiry submission (Inquiry Form -> Supabase -> Auto-Email -> WhatsApp Direct).
- **Cost:** $0/month total infrastructure running cost on Vercel Free Tier.
